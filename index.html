Door

They showed up. Here's what to do.

Start Understand Document Respond Done

First contact — Tex. Fam. Code § 261.307

The department must identify itself, give you a written summary, and tell you your verbal rights. You do not have to fill the silence.

Status: Not set

Script to say

"I am not consenting to entry. I am not answering questions without counsel. Please give me the written summary required by Texas Family Code section 261.307, including the known allegations, and leave your name and identification. I will record this contact."

What they must give you

  • A brief written summary in a language you understand
  • How DFPS investigates, including when it would ask a court to remove a child
  • That every report is sent to law enforcement
  • That anything you say may be used against you — in a criminal case, to remove a child, or to terminate parental rights
  • Your rights to complain, review records (with limits), and seek counsel
  • The verbal rights listed below
  • The known allegations DFPS is investigating

Your verbal rights — § 261.307(a)(2)

  • Do not speak without counsel: Refuse to speak with any DFPS agent unless legal counsel is present.
  • Assistance from an attorney: Right to receive assistance from an attorney.
  • Appointed counsel in some suits: If you cannot afford a lawyer and DFPS seeks a court order under § 262.101, § 262.105, or required services under § 264.203, you may have court-appointed attorney.
  • Record the contact: May record any interaction or interview; recording may be disclosed under court order.
  • Refuse entry without a court order: May refuse entry to home or interview child without court order.
  • Counsel before entry or interview: May have legal counsel present before allowing entry or interview.
  • Withhold medical records consent: May withhold consent to release of medical or mental health records.
  • Withhold exam consent: May withhold consent to any medical or psychological examination of the child.
  • Refuse a drug test: May refuse to submit to a drug test.
  • Counsel before a safety plan: May consult with legal counsel before agreeing to any proposed voluntary safety plan.

Hearing prep checklist

  • Petition, affidavit, and every order are in one stack
  • Counsel is confirmed, or I asked the court for appointed counsel
  • Door card shows whether they gave § 261.307 notice
  • Two-fact log is current — no extra story
  • Photo or copy of any court order they showed
  • I can say whether the child may have Native American heritage
  • Names of relatives or others who can take the child if asked
  • I will not testify or explain without counsel
